궁합 check: are you a match?

Pick your animal and a friend's. 궁합 (goong-hahp) means "how well two people fit."

Every year has a color, too

Look at the last digit of the year. Koreans name each year by color + animal, using the five traditional colors (오방색).

The animal changes at 설날

Seollal (suhl-lahl) is the Korean Lunar New Year.

    Korea's winter season

    From the longest night to the Lunar New Year.

    Dec 22

    동지 Dongji

    The longest night. Red-bean porridge (팥죽, paht-jook) chases bad luck away.

    Dec 25

    크리스마스 Christmas

    A public holiday. Friends and couples share a bakery Christmas cake.

    Dec 31

    제야의 종 The midnight bell

    At midnight, Seoul's Bosingak bell rings 33 times to welcome the new year.

    Feb 7, 2027

    설날 Seollal

    Lunar New Year: bows, rice-cake soup, and a brand-new animal year.
