People and access

Everyone signs in with their own passcode, locked to their UNG email unless you turn the lock off. Add a person, pick their level, and give them the passcode this page makes. After they sign in, they can pick their own username and use it instead of their email. You can change a level, make a new passcode, pause or remove someone at any time; it takes effect within half a minute.

Shared passcodes (Cloudflare)

  • Your master passcode (PLAN_EDIT_PASSCODE) is always an Editor and can't be removed here. Keep it to yourself; it's how you get back in if anything goes wrong.
  • The coordinators' passcode (PLAN_PASSCODE, Viewer) and the faculty passcode (FACULTY_PASSCODE) still work for anyone who has them, without an email and without a name. They can't save anything, so they're fine to keep as backups: stop handing them out once everyone has their own passcode, and keep them in your password manager.
  • If a shared passcode may have leaked, or someone who knew it leaves, change its value in Cloudflare (Worker › Settings › Variables and Secrets › edit the secret); the old one stops working right away. To switch one off for good, delete it there.

Saving events and lab instructions
