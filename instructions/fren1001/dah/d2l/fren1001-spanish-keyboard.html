Spanish keyboard: setup and practice

Type Spanish accents

Choose your computer. Set up once, then practice until the accents feel easy.

On a phone or iPad: press and hold a letter to choose its accent.

Add the keyboard (one time)

  1. Open Start › Settings › Time & language › Language & region. (Windows 10: Settings › Time & Language › Language.)
  2. Next to English (United States), choose … and then Language options. (Windows 10: select it and choose Options.)
  3. Under Keyboards, choose Add a keyboard, then United States-International.

Switch keyboards

Press Windows + Space, or click ENG on the taskbar and choose INTL. Your letters stay in the same places.
