Lab 1: Tout sur moi

  • Due date in D2L
  • Ch. 1
  • Mode Camera
  • Length 1.5–2 min

This is your first conversation in French with your class. Answer each question in your own words.

Your task

A · Bonjour !

  • Greet your audience: Comment t'appelles-tu ?
  • Your age: Quel âge as-tu ?
  • Your nationality or where you're from: D'où es-tu ?
  • 1 reason you study French: Pourquoi est-ce que tu étudies le français ?

B · Qui es-tu ?

  • 5 different adjectives from the Chapter 1 list: Comment es-tu ?

C · Deux questions

  • End with 2 questions for your audience.

Every video

  • 1.5–2 minutes of French.
  • Your face is visible and your voice is clear the whole time.
  • French only, in complete sentences.
  • Don't read from a script.
  • No translators, AI tools, or outside help.

Watch your recording before you submit: your face and voice have to come through. The rubric is in the Bongo assignment.

Record in Bongo

  1. Open the Bongo assignment and choose Camera. Allow the camera and microphone.
  2. Record. Then watch it: check that your face and voice come through.
  3. Submit. To redo it before the due date, open the ⋮ menu and choose Reset Submission.

Problems? See Troubleshooting.

Troubleshooting

Before you record

  • Use Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop. iPads and phones don't work well.
  • Allow the camera and microphone. If you blocked them, click the camera icon in Chrome's address bar.
  • Close Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or anything else using your camera.

The recorder won't load

  1. Clear Chrome's cache and cookies, then reload the page.
  2. Try a Chrome Incognito window.
  3. If the D2L home page shows up inside Bongo, open it in Chrome on a computer.
  4. Restart your computer, then try again.

Other things that stop a recording

  • Windows blocks the camera: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera, and turn on Let desktop apps access your camera. Do the same under Microphone.
  • No sound: AirPods and Bluetooth headphones can take over the microphone. Pick your microphone in Bongo's settings, or disconnect them.
  • Don't close the tab until Bongo finishes uploading. Slow Wi-Fi makes this take longer.
  • Plug in your laptop. Low-power mode and sleep can stop a recording.

Still failing? Record outside Bongo

Record one continuous take with your phone or your computer's camera app. Then choose Upload Video in the same Bongo assignment before 11:59 PM. Videos with cuts, joined clips or replaced audio get 0.

Redo your recording

Open the ⋮ menu at the top right of the assignment and choose Reset Submission. You can do this until the due time.

Your responsibility

Making your lab work is your responsibility: your computer, camera, microphone and Bongo, and a video in the required format. Complete Lab 0 in the first week, start each lab early, and watch your recording before you submit.

  • Only videos submitted in Bongo are graded, not videos sent through D2L, by email, or as a link.
  • The Language Lab doesn't extend a deadline for a technology problem, even one reported before 11:59 PM.
  • If the Language Lab or UNG confirms that Bongo or D2L is down for everyone, or a campus closes, the Lab extends the lab for all sections and posts an announcement.

Still stuck?

Try the Bongo Help Center. Then use the backup for your kind of lab, or come to your campus Language Lab well before the due time. The Lab is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, so don't wait for the evening of the due date.
