Lab Requirement Summary

  • 6 labs
  • 20 points each
  • Due Wednesdays, 11:59 PM (dates in D2L)

FREN 1001 is 4 credit hours: 3 in class and 1 in the Language Lab. The lab is 6 short speaking videos you record in Bongo.

How this folder works

  1. Complete Lab 0 in the first week: pass the Guidelines Quiz (75% or higher; unlimited tries), then record the one-minute Bongo setup check. Together they unlock the labs.
  2. For each lab, record the Lab Bongo assignment. Its instructions are inside Bongo.

You can record at home or in the Language Lab (see Record in the Lab). Set up French accents before you start: see Accents.

Every video

  • 1.5–2 minutes of French.
  • Your face is visible and your voice is clear the whole time.
  • French only, in complete sentences.
  • Don't read from a script.
  • No translators, AI tools, or outside help.

Watch your recording before you submit: your face and voice have to come through. The rubric is in the Bongo assignment.

Academic honesty

Every lab must be your own work, in your own words.

  • Not allowed: online translators, AI tools (for example ChatGPT or Google Translate), help from another person, or reading from a script.
  • Allowed while you prepare: Class notes, Bonjour à Tous, and WordReference.com.

Breaking these rules is academic misconduct. It can mean a 0 on the lab and other consequences from the Office of Student Conduct. You agree to this in the Guidelines Quiz.

Deadlines and technology

Labs are due Wednesdays by 11:59 PM. Each lab's date is in D2L. Late work is not accepted.

Making your lab work is your responsibility: your computer, camera, microphone and Bongo, and a video in the required format. Complete Lab 0 in the first week, start each lab early, and watch your recording before you submit. If Bongo fails, follow Troubleshooting.

Missed a lab because of an excused absence? Contact your professor.

Approved accommodations through Student Accessibility Services (SAS)? Contact your professor and your campus Language Lab coordinator at the start of the semester. The coordinator works with SAS directly.

Grades

Language Lab staff grade the labs, not your professor, usually within a week. Send grade questions to the Language Lab.

Changes to dates or instructions are sent to your UNG email.

Accents

On a phone or iPad: press and hold a letter to choose its accent.

Add the keyboard (one time)

  1. Open Start › Settings › Time & language › Language & region. (Windows 10: Settings › Time & Language › Language.)
  2. Next to English (United States), choose … and then Language options. (Windows 10: select it and choose Options.)
  3. Under Keyboards, choose Add a keyboard, then United States-International.

Switch keyboards

Press Windows + Space, or click ENG on the taskbar and choose INTL. Your letters stay in the same places.

Troubleshooting

For Labs 1, 2, 3 and 6.

Before you record

  • Use Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop. iPads and phones don't work well.
  • Allow the camera and microphone. If you blocked them, click the camera icon in Chrome's address bar.
  • Close Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or anything else using your camera.

The recorder won't load

  1. Clear Chrome's cache and cookies, then reload the page.
  2. Try a Chrome Incognito window.
  3. If the D2L home page shows up inside Bongo, open it in Chrome on a computer.
  4. Restart your computer, then try again.

Other things that stop a recording

  • Windows blocks the camera: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera, and turn on Let desktop apps access your camera. Do the same under Microphone.
  • No sound: AirPods and Bluetooth headphones can take over the microphone. Pick your microphone in Bongo's settings, or disconnect them.
  • Don't close the tab until Bongo finishes uploading. Slow Wi-Fi makes this take longer.
  • Plug in your laptop. Low-power mode and sleep can stop a recording.

Still failing? Record outside Bongo

Record one continuous take with your phone or your computer's camera app. Then choose Upload Video in the same Bongo assignment before 11:59 PM. Videos with cuts, joined clips or replaced audio get 0.

Redo your recording

Open the ⋮ menu at the top right of the assignment and choose Reset Submission. For separate prompts: open the ⋮ menu next to the prompt and choose Retake. You can do this until the due time.

For Lab 0, Labs 4 and 5.

Before you record

  • Use Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop. iPads and phones don't work well.
  • Allow the camera and microphone, and screen sharing. If you blocked them, click the camera icon in Chrome's address bar.
  • Close Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or anything else using your camera.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb and close email and other tabs, so nothing private shows in your video.

The recorder won't load

  1. Clear Chrome's cache and cookies, then reload the page.
  2. Try a Chrome Incognito window.
  3. If the D2L home page shows up inside Bongo, open it in Chrome on a computer.
  4. Restart your computer, then try again.

Sharing your screen

Mac: Chrome can't share the screen, or you only see the wallpaper. macOS blocks screen sharing until you allow it:

  1. Click the Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Click Screen & System Audio Recording (on older Macs: Screen Recording).
  3. Turn on Google Chrome. If it isn't listed, click + and choose Chrome from Applications.
  4. Choose Quit & Reopen. Chrome must restart before sharing works.
  5. Open the Bongo assignment again and share your slides' window.

In the same Privacy & Security list, check that Chrome is also on under Camera and Microphone. Apple's guide

  • Share the window with your slides, not the Bongo tab. PowerPoint online and Canva can be shared as a Chrome tab.
  • Your slideshow isn't in the list? A full-screen slideshow can't be picked. In PowerPoint, use View → Reading View.
  • Two monitors? Share the screen that has your slides.

Other things that stop a recording

  • Windows blocks the camera: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera, and turn on Let desktop apps access your camera. Do the same under Microphone.
  • No sound: AirPods and Bluetooth headphones can take over the microphone. Pick your microphone in Bongo's settings, or disconnect them.
  • Don't close the tab until Bongo finishes uploading. Slow Wi-Fi makes this take longer.
  • Plug in your laptop. Low-power mode and sleep can stop a recording.

Still failing? Record your slides in PowerPoint or Canva

Only a PowerPoint or Canva recording, exported as an MP4, is accepted. Other screen recorders and edited videos aren't graded. Upload it to the same Bongo assignment before 11:59 PM.

PowerPoint (free with your UNG account):

  1. Go to office.com, sign in with your UNG email, and open your slides (or use the desktop app).
  2. Open the Record tab and choose Record from Beginning with the camera on.
  3. Talk through your slides, then click Stop.
  4. File → Export → Export to Video (720p, MP4).
  5. In Bongo, choose Upload Video and select the file.

Canva: build your slides at canva.com, choose Present → Present and record, download the MP4, and upload it in Bongo.

Both record slide by slide. That's fine: upload the video as exported, with no other editing.

Redo your recording

Open the ⋮ menu at the top right of the assignment and choose Reset Submission. You can do this until the due time.

Lab 0 and accents

  • Accents won't type? On Windows, check that the taskbar says INTL. Setup steps are in Accents.
