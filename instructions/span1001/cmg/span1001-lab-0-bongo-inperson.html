Lab 0: Bongo setup check

  • Due in the first week
  • Mode Camera + Screen
  • Length about 1 min
  • Tries unlimited
  • Grade 0%

Lab 0 checks that your computer can record your camera and your screen at the same time. That is the hardest kind of lab to record, so if Lab 0 works, every lab will. Do it in the first week, so there's time to fix a problem.

This is part 2 of Lab 0. Part 1 is the Guidelines Quiz.

Make 3 PowerPoint slides

Keywords or pictures only, no sentences. PowerPoint is free with your UNG account at office.com.

  1. Your name
  2. Your course and your professor's name
  3. One thing you like (a picture is fine)

Record in Bongo

  1. Open your slides in their own window. Desktop PowerPoint: View → Reading View. PowerPoint online: start the slide show in its own Chrome tab.
  2. Open Lab 0 in Bongo and choose Camera + Screen. Allow the camera, the microphone, and screen sharing.
  3. Share the window (or tab) with your slides, not the Bongo tab.
  4. Talk through your slides for about a minute. Start with Hola, me llamo… and your name. The rest can be in English.
  5. Watch your recording, then Submit.

Problems? See Troubleshooting. If screen sharing still fails, record your slides in PowerPoint and upload the MP4: that's also what you'd do if a slide lab fails on a due date.

What the Lab checks

A Lab assistant marks it Complete or Not yet (with a comment) within one business day. Not yet? Fix the problem and submit again. Lab 0 stays open all semester, so you can test a new computer any time.

Your labs unlock when you've passed the Guidelines Quiz and this check is marked Complete.

Troubleshooting

Before you record

  • Use Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop. iPads and phones don't work well.
  • Allow the camera and microphone, and screen sharing. If you blocked them, click the camera icon in Chrome's address bar.
  • Close Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, or anything else using your camera.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb and close email and other tabs, so nothing private shows in your video.

The recorder won't load

  1. Clear Chrome's cache and cookies, then reload the page.
  2. Try a Chrome Incognito window.
  3. If the D2L home page shows up inside Bongo, open it in Chrome on a computer.
  4. Restart your computer, then try again.

Sharing your screen

Mac: Chrome can't share the screen, or you only see the wallpaper. macOS blocks screen sharing until you allow it:

  1. Click the Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Click Screen & System Audio Recording (on older Macs: Screen Recording).
  3. Turn on Google Chrome. If it isn't listed, click + and choose Chrome from Applications.
  4. Choose Quit & Reopen. Chrome must restart before sharing works.
  5. Open the Bongo assignment again and share your slides' window.

In the same Privacy & Security list, check that Chrome is also on under Camera and Microphone. Apple's guide

  • Share the window with your slides, not the Bongo tab. PowerPoint online and Canva can be shared as a Chrome tab.
  • Your slideshow isn't in the list? A full-screen slideshow can't be picked. In PowerPoint, use View → Reading View.
  • Two monitors? Share the screen that has your slides.

Other things that stop a recording

  • Windows blocks the camera: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera, and turn on Let desktop apps access your camera. Do the same under Microphone.
  • No sound: AirPods and Bluetooth headphones can take over the microphone. Pick your microphone in Bongo's settings, or disconnect them.
  • Don't close the tab until Bongo finishes uploading. Slow Wi-Fi makes this take longer.
  • Plug in your laptop. Low-power mode and sleep can stop a recording.

Still failing? Record your slides in PowerPoint or Canva

Only a PowerPoint or Canva recording, exported as an MP4, is accepted. Other screen recorders and edited videos aren't graded. Upload it to the same Bongo assignment before 5:00 PM.

PowerPoint (free with your UNG account):

  1. Go to office.com, sign in with your UNG email, and open your slides (or use the desktop app).
  2. Open the Record tab and choose Record from Beginning with the camera on.
  3. Talk through your slides, then click Stop.
  4. File → Export → Export to Video (720p, MP4).
  5. In Bongo, choose Upload Video and select the file.

Canva: build your slides at canva.com, choose Present → Present and record, download the MP4, and upload it in Bongo.

Both record slide by slide. That's fine: upload the video as exported, with no other editing.

Redo your recording

Open the ⋮ menu at the top right of the assignment and choose Reset Submission. You can do this until the due time.

Your responsibility

Making your lab work is your responsibility: your computer, camera, microphone and Bongo, and a video in the required format (face visible, voice clear, and slides shown in slide labs). Complete Lab 0 in the first week, start each lab early, and watch your recording before you submit. A technology problem never extends the deadline, even if you report it before the due time.

  • Only videos submitted in Bongo are graded, not videos sent through D2L, by email, or as a link.
  • The Language Lab doesn't extend a deadline for a technology problem, even one reported before 5:00 PM.
  • If the Language Lab or UNG confirms that Bongo or D2L is down for everyone, or a campus closes, the Lab extends the lab for all sections and posts an announcement.

Still stuck?

Try the Bongo Help Center. Then use the backup for your kind of lab, or come to your campus Language Lab well before the due time. Contacting the Lab doesn't extend the deadline.
