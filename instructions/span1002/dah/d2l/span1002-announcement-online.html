SPAN 1002 includes 6 short speaking labs. Everything is in one place: Content › LAB.

Open the LAB folderTake the Guidelines Quiz

Get started

  1. Open Content › LAB and read the folder description, starting with Requirements.
  2. Set up Spanish accents on your computer. Steps are in the Quizzes & accents tab.
  3. Complete Lab 0: pass the Guidelines Quiz (75%), then record the one-minute Bongo setup check. Together they unlock Lab 1.
  4. For each lab: take the Mini-Quiz, then record the Bongo assignment.
  • All labs Fridays, 5:00 PM (dates in D2L)
  • Late work not accepted
