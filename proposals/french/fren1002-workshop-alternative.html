Workshop alternative form

Fill in each part of this form when your lab's workshop alternative page says to. The steps are on that page.

How to fill it in

  1. Open it in a program that fills in forms: Adobe Acrobat Reader (free, Windows, Mac, phone), Preview on a Mac, or Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
  2. Click a box and type. Accents: use your French keyboard (see Accents in the LAB folder).
  3. Save the file with the Save or Download button, not Print. Reopen it to check that your answers are still there.
  4. Upload it with your video to the lab's Workshop dropbox in D2L.
