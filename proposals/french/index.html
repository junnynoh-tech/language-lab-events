Summary

Language Lab proposal: FREN 1001 and FREN 1002

The French labs keep their content and platforms (Bongo for FREN 1001, TalkAbroad for FREN 1002). What changes is how students find the instructions, a setup check in week one, and a set of fixes where the current pages disagree with each other.

Open the FREN 1001 proposalOpen the FREN 1002 proposal

What changes, in one minute

  1. One LAB folderOne folder per course with a tabbed description, so the rules, labs, accents and contact are in one place.
  2. Lab 0 in week oneA Guidelines Quiz that unlocks the labs. FREN 1001 adds a one-minute Bongo camera and screen check; FREN 1002 adds TalkAbroad setup. Both 0%.
  3. Typing FrenchA keyboard demo for Windows and Mac, with typing practice and three typed accent questions in the quiz.
  4. FREN 1001: requirements in plain sightOn each Bongo page, always visible and with counts, instead of hidden in drop-downs. Lab 4 and Lab 5 work for Gainesville, Dahlonega or the student's own town.
  5. FREN 1001 Lab 6: 10 recorded questionsA Bongo Question & Answer with 10 questions recorded by a French professor, 2–3 minutes in all, in place of picking 5 of 47.
  6. FREN 1002: vocabulary from Top HatLists rebuilt from the Top Hat chapter lists, with 18 Top Hat errors corrected and listed for you to confirm. Each word links to Forvo for audio.
  7. FREN 1002: due Fridays at 5:00 PMWas 3:00 PM; now the same time as SPAN 1001 and SPAN 1002.
  8. FREN 1002: workshops in person or onlineLab assistants run every workshop. Online sections get online workshops, so Gainesville and Dahlonega can share lab assistants.
  9. Consistent pagesFixes where pages disagreed: video length, points (FREN 1001: 20, scored with the rubric), resources, Lab 5's title and grading in FREN 1002.

What stays the same

  • FREN 1001: six labs, individual Bongo videos, no mini-quizzes, the five-row rubric, due Wednesdays at 11:59 PM.
  • FREN 1002: TalkAbroad conversations with a preparation document and an mp3, the TalkAbroad rubric (25 points per lab).
  • FREN 1002 summer sections: due Wednesdays at 11:59 PM.

What we'd like from you

A short reply to the email is plenty. These are the questions we most need answered:

  1. FREN 1001 Lab 6: is the 10-question Bongo Question & Answer all right?
  2. FREN 1002 Top Hat corrections: could you confirm them? They're listed by chapter in the Top Hat corrections tab.
  3. FREN 1002 summer: should summer sections add workshops, or stay without them?
  4. Due times: FREN 1001 stays Wednesday 11:59 PM and FREN 1002 moves to Friday 5:00 PM. Keep them different, or align them?
  5. When you copy your course next semester, could you leave the lab items out? The steps are in the Course copy tab.

How to review the full proposals

  • 1. Start with “What changes.” Each tile shows before and after. Click a tile to see why.
  • 2. Use the menu on the left. Everything under Student pages is the real page students would see: the announcement, the LAB folder, Lab 0, each lab.
  • 3. Use the switches. The buttons above a preview switch between labs and between in-person and online sections. They stay in reach as you scroll.

Works best on a laptop or desktop. Dates in the previews are this semester's, as an example. The buttons at the top switch between the summary and each course.
