Summary

Language Lab proposal: SPAN 1001 and SPAN 1002

The same six labs and Bongo recordings, organized so students find everything in one place, test their setup in week one, and are graded on one speaking rubric.

Open the SPAN 1001 proposalOpen the SPAN 1002 proposal

What changes, in one minute

  1. One LAB folderEverything students need sits in one folder per course: a tabbed description, Lab 0, then each lab's mini-quiz and Bongo recording. No Start Here module, no subfolders.
  2. Lab 0 in week oneThe Guidelines Quiz plus a one-minute Bongo camera and screen check, both 0%. Recording problems show up in week one instead of on a due date.
  3. One due time: 5:00 PMBoth courses. SPAN 1001 labs were due Fridays at 3:00 PM; 5:00 PM is still during Lab hours, so students can get help before it closes.
  4. One speaking rubric, still 25 pointsFluency & Discourse 10 · Comprehensibility & Accuracy 5 · Vocabulary 5 · Task 5. Course weights don't change.
  5. Better tech helpTroubleshooting for video labs and slide labs, each with one backup students can finish before the deadline: a phone recording, or a PowerPoint or Canva MP4.
  6. SPAN 1002: one video per labOne recording per lab with every question written on the Bongo page, instead of Question & Answer prompts heard one at a time.
  7. No face or no audio = 0In both courses, with no resubmission, as SPAN 1002 does now. Lab 0 has already tested the student's setup.

What stays the same

  • Six labs (four in summer), 25 points each, graded by Language Lab staff.
  • Each lab is a mini-quiz plus a Bongo recording: 1.5–2 minutes, face visible, Spanish only.
  • The Guidelines Quiz (75%) still unlocks the labs, now together with Lab 0.

What we'd like from you

A short reply to the email is plenty. These are the questions we most need answered:

  1. Does the new rubric work for your course?
  2. SPAN 1001: is moving the due time from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM all right?
  3. When you copy your course next semester, could you leave the lab items out? The Lab sets up a fresh LAB folder. The steps, with pictures, are in the Course copy tab of each proposal.
  4. Is anything students see unclear? Every tab shows the real student page.

How to review the full proposals

  • 1. Start with “What changes.” Each tile shows before and after. Click a tile to see why.
  • 2. Use the menu on the left. Everything under Student pages is the real page students would see: the announcement, the LAB folder, Lab 0, each lab.
  • 3. Use the switches. The buttons above a preview switch between labs and between in-person and online sections. They stay in reach as you scroll.

Works best on a laptop or desktop. Dates in the previews are this semester's, as an example. The buttons at the top switch between the summary and each course.
